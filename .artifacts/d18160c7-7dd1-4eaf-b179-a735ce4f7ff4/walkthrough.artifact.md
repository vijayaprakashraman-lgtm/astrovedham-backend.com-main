# Walkthrough - Sacred Emerald & Gold Theme Update

The application's visual identity has been completely refreshed to follow the new "Sacred Emerald & Gold" theme, providing a more premium, celestial, and traditional feel.

## Key Color Transformations

### Brand & Accents
- **Primary Brand Color**: Shifted to **Deep Emerald** (`#064E3B`).
- **Sacred Accents**: Updated to **Sacred Gold** (`#D97706`) and **Accent Gold** (`#FFB300`).
- **Auspicious Indicators**: Success states now use a vibrant **Success Green** (`#10B981`).
- **Alert Indicators**: Error states now use a clear **Alert Red** (`#DC2626`).

### Surfaces & Backgrounds
- **Main Background**: Now uses a soft **Mint Cream** (`#ECFDF5`), replacing the previous clinical white.
- **Surface Elevation**: Cards and elevated panels now use a refined **Jade Green** (`#0F766E`).
- **Borders & Dividers**: Updated to a very light mint (`#D1FAE5`) for a subtle, professional look.

### Typography
- **Primary Text**: Now uses a deep, readable **Forest Green** (`#022C22`).
- **Secondary Text**: Now uses a lighter **Sacred Emerald** (`#047857`).

## Theme-Specific Updates

### Light Mode
- The app now feels warmer and more integrated with the celestial theme due to the **Mint Cream background**.
- Buttons and interactive elements pop with the **Deep Emerald** and **Sacred Gold** combination.

### Dark Mode
- Updated to a **Deep Forest** palette (`#022C22` background).
- Text in dark mode now uses the **Light Mint Cream** (`#ECFDF5`) for maximum contrast and legibility.

## Verification Summary

- **Component Check**: Verified that `ElevatedButton`, `NavigationBar`, and `HoroscopeServiceCard` all inherit the new semantic color mappings.
- **Contrast Check**: Deep green text on Mint Cream background meets high legibility standards.
- **Visual Flow**: The transition from Home to Service details remains seamless under the new palette.

> [!TIP]
> The specialized functional colors (Auspicious Green, Inauspicious Red, and Informational Blue) are now available in `AppColors` for use in upcoming features like Muhurtham and Rahu Kalam markers.
